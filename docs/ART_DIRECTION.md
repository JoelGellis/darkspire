# Darkspire Art Direction

## Core fantasy

Darkspire is gothic, dangerous, ancient, and beautiful: a cursed vertical fortress built over centuries by kings, cults, prisoners, and things that should have stayed buried.

Outside a run, evoke Darkest Dungeon: torchlit, painterly, gothic town and campfire scenes. Inside a run, retain Slay-the-Spire-like tactical clarity: party and enemy ranks, cards, intent, and targets must read instantly.

Joel's 2026-09-05 refinement: these are close **layout references**, not merely palette references. Campfire selection uses Diablo-like full-body selectable figures; town follows the Darkest Dungeon hamlet arrangement (painted buildings, roster on the right, embark below); runs follow Slay the Spire's parchment routes, overhead intent, bottom card hand, left energy/draw and right end-turn/discard. Reuse Darkspire's own character and environment exports across the whole loop. Do not fabricate extra mechanics or empty controls just to mimic a reference screenshot.

## Visual pillars

1. **Gothic verticality.** Tall arches, towers, stairs, spires, chains, narrow bridges, shafts, and moonlit silhouettes.
2. **Cursed elegance.** Beauty corrupted by ash, rust, bone, wax, thorns, dried blood, and black iron.
3. **Readable darkness.** Darkness frames play; it never obscures gameplay. Interactables use controlled highlights and strong silhouettes.
4. **Ritual horror, not gore spam.** Favor candles, sigils, veils, relics, bells, masks, ravens, smoke, and impossible architecture. Gore is rare and meaningful.
5. **Expensive minimalism.** Fewer better assets. Each scene has one clear focal point rather than indiscriminate clutter.

## Palette

- Primary: near-black blue, charcoal, desaturated purple, bone white, tarnished silver, dried-blood red.
- Accents: candle gold, cursed green, moonlit cyan, royal crimson.
- Avoid: bright cartoon colors, clean generic-fantasy stone, plastic-looking metal, and skull clutter without purpose.

## Shape and lighting

Architecture is tall, narrow, pointed, and initially symmetrical. Enemies are hunched, elongated, masked, antlered, veiled, or decayed beneath armor. The player needs a compact, readable silhouette with a clear weapon and cloak/coat motion.

Every scene needs one dominant light source, a secondary rim or bounce light, a readable gameplay plane, and restrained fog or particles.

## Acceptance standard

Before accepting an asset:

- Its silhouette reads at gameplay size.
- Value contrast works in grayscale.
- It belongs in the Darkspire palette and shape language.
- It has no AI artifacts or unreadable micro-detail.
- Its editable source and runtime export are both saved in the correct pipeline locations.

## Naming

Use lowercase kebab-case, for example `enemy-bell-wraith.png`, `prop-rusted-candelabra.blend`, and `tileset-cathedral-prison.png`.
