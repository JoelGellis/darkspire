Contract and unresolved planner conflicts

Four heroes, eight classes, no stress, hard rank gates, permanent character death, gear dies with its wearer. Keep four personal slots, at most two equipped items per hero at embark, two party artifacts. Ownership breadth may grow to the proposed 55 definitions (17 existing +32 personal +6 artifacts); embark capacity never grows with merchant level. Finish the other eight slots during expeditions. Free field fitting occurs only between encounters.

Planner1 proposes one prepared item action per hero; planner2 proposes two. For the first economy experiment use ONE prepared action per hero, with other equipped actions explicitly sheathed, no loss of passives. This is a proposed resolution requiring runtime/UI/save implementation, not a claim about current behavior. It limits the 32-card start to36 with four injected actions. A one-copy payoff has opening visibility5/36=13.89%, versus12.5% at40. Preserve existing saves and show a preparation choice on migration; do not silently erase cards. Cleric remains launch ranks2/3. This plan adds no new class cards or item definitions beyond the specialist catalogs.

Gold purchases buy lasting ownership, survivor-bound learning or permanent wound removal. Found drafts, potions, blessings and camp preparation can expire. No gold-bought potion, one-fight buff or run-only card. Permanent value is still exposed to the stated death/exit risks. Purchased learning on a surviving hero remains after flee. Flee physical-gear provenance is pending root reconciliation: planner2 loses all run acquisitions, while the expedition implementer retains purchased gear on surviving wearers and loses found unbanked loot. Recommend the latter to preserve gold-purchase and wearer rules; do not implement conflicting outcomes independently. Show the resolved rule before purchase and in flee confirmation.

Three-act route grammar

Acts: Broken Watch (act1), Rootbound Ossuary (act2), Ashen Court (act3), matching planner3 pools. Each act has start, seven decision depths, final camp, boss: ten displayed rows including start. All fit a fixed viewport with horizontal/vertical world camera within the map pane if needed; never document scroll. Each depth has2–3 nodes. No backtracking or paid map rerolls. A route chooses one node per depth, with at most one optional inserted treasure stop.

Cards, training and permanence

Free fight drafts remain expedition-only, one chosen owner-specific non-base-kit card from3. Three distinct definitions; no gear/generated/curse/base-kit definitions. At least two living owners represented when available; one offer for least-represented owner's active deck when legal. Each selected card gets stable heroID/source/instance. Duplicate-class heroes require explicit owner selection. Two-copy cap by owner+definition. If insufficient candidates, show fewer and skip, never fake diversity. Print deck-size delta and owner share. No forced reward acceptance.

Dungeon gold card shelf becomes lesson license: common50, uncommon65, rare75. Purchase teaches selected living hero that class-valid non-base action persistently, adds one copy this run, and allows at most ONE learned elective in that hero's next starting stack in place of one ordinary non-signature base action. Original rolled kit is preserved as immutable record; electives cannot overwrite signature identity and can be unequipped at campfire. Known licenses are unlimited; only one elective can be prepared per hero, so capacity limits preparation rather than ownership. No town card sales. License survives with hero, dies with hero, never transfers through gear. Acquisition nonce makes repeat purchase of known license illegal.

Paid removal75 becomes Discipline: one permanent slot per hero permitting one ordinary base action to be left unprepared on future embark; immediately remove one eligible non-signature run card. Maximum one Discipline per hero, no repeated scaling. It may not remove a curse through this endpoint or reduce base prepared stack below7; elective replaces rather than stacks. First run with four disciplined heroes can start28base+4item=32, a300g opportunity cost across four heroes, not free thinning. In-run found curse cleansing stays earned. UI calls this purchased preparation training, explaining future effect.

Paid mastery50 upgrades one selected eligible base-kit instance on living hero permanently through canonical upgrade definition, once per instance and subject to existing class mastery cap. Learned non-base cards may instead buy persistent upgrade for65 once. Do not stack paid level/mastery bonuses twice. Free camp training remains run-only and is labeled temporary. No free draft laundering into permanent library through upgrade, transformation or item source mutation. Wound treatment remains existing permanent heal-cost service; no purchased expedition HP restore. All persistent learning edits are journaled immediately with heroID and death cleanup, not inferred from final deck.

Objectives, contracts, transition and ascension

Select at most one optional objective before departure, no entry fee. Seal the Watch: defeat act1elite and boss without fleeing, earn permanent Watch Bell if unowned or38g salvage. Rescue Dispatch: complete Courier before act2boss, earn60g on successful settlement (retreat halves gold, flee loses it). Broken Ritual: interrupt two distinct channels then defeat act2boss, earn one compatible rare gear choice at settlement. Hold the Line: defeat all3bosses with at least3survivors, unlock one hero elective license for a surviving eligible hero, no duplicate buyback. Objectives evaluate logged authoritative events and require stated endpoint; progress itself never pays repeatedly. No objective asks for pointless long fights or number-of-clicks grinding.

Expedition contract progression: Scouting available immediately, voluntary retreat allowed anywhere, tutorial protection applies only first departed expedition; Descent unlocks after act1boss, enables objective selection; Crown Hunt unlocks after first full3act victory and enables ascension. These are campaign unlocks, not fees or mandatory repeat grinds. Scouting may still complete all acts; unlocking does not truncate a run or make act1boss equal full victory.

Final camp choose exactly one: heal30%maxHP living heroes; free temporary card upgrade; remove one selected persistent curse; or ward6block each first boss turn. No automatic heal after boss, no revival, no resetting equipped-item encounter limits except normal new-combat reset.

Act1/2 transition choose one next-act-only blessing: Hasty March, opening energy+1 but draw-1; Long Counsel, opening draw+2 but energy-1; Triage, heal each survivor8 now, no blessing. Applies first turn of every combat in next act; discard prior transition blessing. Base3energy/5draw becomes4/4 or2/7 before other explicit bonuses. Individual artifact bonuses then add; hand cap10. Triage does not resurrect. These are earned temporary choices with explicit drawbacks, planner3 ID69 refined. Add a separate permanent reward at each transition: choose one compatible uncommon (act1) /rare (act2) gear from3 or exact salvage; this fulfills powerful permanent build progression without charging gold for aura. Include these two extra gear rewards in availability reporting; raw588gold arithmetic intentionally excludes their salvage value.

Ascensions cumulative, selected before seed generation: A1 normals maxHP ceil(base*1.10); A2 elites add1total direct damage per enemy action (single hit+1, multihit/AoE one point to first deterministic target/hit, never per-hit multiplication); A3 bosses add3 to their first block-producing action per phase, no new untelegraphed action. Unlock A(n+1) only full expedition win at current highest unlocked n. No rewards multiplier initially, no changed AI information access, no hidden randomness advantage. Modifiers and adjusted intents visible. Higher A4+ is later round, not three copy-pasted HP increases now.

Settlement, saves and retained run data

One campaign revision transaction has runId+settlementId+outcome, immutable before/after ledger and applied marker. All handlers consume that record; render is pure. Use stable hero IDs, never roster indices after deaths. Snapshot carried gear origin (town/run), final wearer and stash; list each retained/lost/sold definition and reason. Campaign and run stores need a recoverable transaction journal: prepare validated result, persist journal, apply campaign revision idempotently, mark run settled, commit journal. On reload reconcile revision; do not apply outcome twice. If persistence fails, freeze settlement and expose export/retry; do not proclaim rewards saved.

Gold ledger: treasury is never exposed by departure. Track goldEarned, goldSpent, goldSalvaged, expedition wallet, stipend separately. Victory banks remaining earned wallet; retreat floor(remaining earned wallet*0.5); flee/wipe0. Selling pre-run gear permanently consumes it and credits earned wallet subject to exit rate; no refund of the sold object. Tutorial escape banks full earned wallet and preserves all deployed heroes/gear, including restored casualty records for that one protected run. No stipend is banked. Proposed reconciliation: purchased physical gear retains on victory/retreat and on a surviving wearer after flee, found run loot loses on flee, and all deployed gear loses on wipe; pre-run gear on surviving flee heroes returns, all worn gear on dead heroes is lost. Stashed pre-run gear returns except an explicitly risked/sold item; untaken bank inventory always safe. Party artifacts are deployed assets: wipe loses them, flee retains pre-run only. Character learning persists only on survivors.

Keep all game diagnostic run events, offered choices, declined choices, transaction attempts and results, build snapshots before/after, purchases/fit/salvage/loss, RNG stream cursors, route and objectives, outcomes and game content version. Player identity mandatory: human name; AI provider/exact model/version/effort with unknown explicit; scripted policy/version and effort not applicable. Full diagnostic RNG is never visible to policy observations. Policy histories retain limits/timeouts/failures. Local archive is not automatic remote collection; do not promise root can see Joel's demo runs until a verified backend/export path exists. Log storage failures rather than silently dropping old runs.
