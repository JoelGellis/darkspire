# Shared-world art review — 2026-09-05

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, then reviewed and processed through the editable SVG/resvg production pipeline. No reference-game artwork was downloaded or incorporated into runtime assets.

## Reference and layout decisions

The [Darkest Dungeon hamlet screenshot](https://www.mobygames.com/game/windows/darkest-dungeon/screenshots/gameShotId%2C818002/) informed the scene-first town, right-side roster and bottom embark action. The [Slay the Spire battle screenshot](https://www.pocketgamer.com/slay-the-spire/ascension-guide/) informed overhead intent, bottom cards and the energy/end-turn arrangement. Darkspire retains its own classes, four-hero rank system, game state and paintings rather than adding nonfunctional copies of reference-game controls.

## Accepted layers and edits

- `hamlet-raw.png`: reviewed for five separate building silhouettes, readable courtyard, distant fortress, depth, no people/text/UI. Chapel left, tavern right, forge lower left, merchant lower right, graveyard middle rear. `../vector/hamlet-environment.svg` reduces saturation to .82, adds editable top/bottom scrims and exports a 1600 × 900 plate. Building anchors were adjusted against the actual browser composition. At phone widths the scene crops laterally and labels remain accessible.
- `dungeon-raw.png`: reviewed for a clear side-view playing plane, centered fire source, unobstructed opposing actor groups and quiet lower card zone. `../vector/dungeon-environment.svg` reduces saturation to .76 and layers a separate blue-black floor scrim, exporting 1600 × 900. It is reused behind map, combat, events, rewards and summaries rather than changing the visual language mid-run.
- `enemies-raw.png`: 1536 × 1024 RGBA, genuine alpha 0 at empty pixels (the tool preview exposes hidden brown RGB). Eight distinct archetypes. Reviewed all cell boundaries, full weapons/legs, silhouettes and scales. `../vector/export-world.py` clips each 384 × 512 cell, adds 8 px padding, reduces saturation to .72 and exports 360 × 500 RGBA. No adjacent-cell fragments or baked checkerboard ship. Browser battle review confirmed transparent compositing and HP/intent readability.
- Reused the earlier eight class exports for campfire figures, party bars, town roster, combat, rest, summary and card art. CSS portrait cropping and absolute document-base card URLs avoid duplicate raster exports.
- Deliberate reuse limitation: skeleton fighters, armored guardians and several humanoid threats share the sentinel; magical enemies share the occultist; beasts share a wolf-like archetype. These are readable families, not a claim of bespoke anatomical accuracy for every named rat, bat or archer. Dedicated enemy variants and individually painted action cards are future production work. No costume micro-detail is relied upon for gameplay.

## Exact generation prompts

### Hamlet

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production background plate for DARKSPIRE, a gothic browser RPG. Wide 16:9 landscape 1536x864. A Darkest Dungeon inspired hamlet composition: elevated view over a compact medieval courtyard, crooked stone and half-timber buildings arranged like a stage. Chapel with tall narrow bell tower upper left, warm tavern upper right, orange-lit blacksmith forge lower left, a covered merchant wagon lower right, small old graveyard and arched gate at top center beneath a distant forbidding fortress. Central courtyard empty with a lonely iron lantern. Style: painterly dark fantasy with assertive ink-black outlines and broad brush shapes, realistic architectural texture simplified for game readability. Black blue, charcoal, bone moonlight, desaturated plum, candle gold in windows. Twilight sky and fog make layers readable, not uniformly black. No people. No text, labels, logos, interface, frames or watermark. Buildings must have distinct readable silhouettes and enough light to identify them at game scale. This will have clickable UI labels over the 5 buildings and a separate roster sidebar outside the image.

### Dungeon

Use case: stylized-concept. A production battle background plate for a gothic dark fantasy card roguelike DARKSPIRE. Wide 16:9 1536x864, eye-level side view, like a theatrical 2D battle stage. NO characters, creatures, UI, cards, text or symbols. A ruined cathedral prison interior, deep pointed arches receding into blue-charcoal mist, ragged burgundy banners, a warm brazier burning on a pillar at center, sparse candles and stone steps. Broad level stone floor across full width at lower 60 percent, completely clear for two opposing groups of full body game sprites. Keep lower quarter dark and visually quiet for a hand of cards overlay. Painted fantasy game art, realistic dark hand painted texture with strong ink-black contours, heavy shadow shapes, worn iron and chipped stone. Palette bone, desaturated purple, black blue, amber firelight. The center should separate silhouettes and left/right actors should be rim lit. Generous playable foreground, not a long perspective corridor swallowing figures. No skull decoration. Crisp architectural silhouettes and controlled detail.

All facing slightly right, weapons fully inside cell. Top row left to right: (1) SENTINEL a towering cursed black-iron armored skeletal knight with sword and shield, (2) GOBLIN a hunched leathery small green-grey goblin with knife and ragged brown hood, (3) OCCULTIST a tall crimson-robed masked undead sorcerer with bone staff, (4) WRAITH a pale veiled spectral humanoid in shredded blue-black robes.
